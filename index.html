About Me

My acedemic interests include linguistics, literature, and philosophy. One of my particular interests is in the way language mediates and forms our relationship with the world and arts role in that process.

I am currently working on a project in Comptation for Linguists, in which I perform a linguistic analysis of a text of my choosing.

Interests

  • Philology
  • Semiotics
